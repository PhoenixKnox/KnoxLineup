Brimstone guides by map

A very effective way to remember Brimstone’s lineups, or reproduce them in 2 seconds: each image is split into 3 parts — Location, Zoomed in (where to aim?) and Landing.

Hover a pin on the map to see its lineup right beside it, click to pin it, then go full screen — on mobile, just tap it. Filter by side, site or setups; every map also has its own guide video, just below.
